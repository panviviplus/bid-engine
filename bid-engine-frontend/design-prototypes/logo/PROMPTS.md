# BidEngine Logo Candidate Prompts

Mode: built-in ImageGen. Use case: `logo-brand`.

## A — Current logo refinement

Input role: the existing `public/bid-engine-logo.ico`, converted to PNG, was the edit target and identity reference.

> Refine the existing BidEngine logo without redesigning its core identity. Preserve the recognizable angular gold letter B geometry and the exact English word “BidEngine”. Enlarge the B symbol and wordmark dramatically, tighten vertical spacing, and reduce empty outer margins so the active artwork fills nearly the entire square canvas. Use crisp vector-like hard edges on a deep navy-black background, with the restrained gold/white identity from the reference. Render “BidEngine” exactly once. No tagline, unrelated symbols, mockup, 3D scene, watermark, tiny centered logo, or excessive padding.

## B — Document-fold B

Base prompt:

> Create an original geometric BidEngine monogram. Build a capital B from the negative space of two stacked document pages with one precise folded page corner. The mark should suggest tender documents being structured and understood without becoming a generic file icon. Use a strong vector-like silhouette, deep navy-black background, warm signal gold, and the exact wordmark “BidEngine”. Keep narrow safe margins and make the mark recognizable at 16px. Avoid checkmarks, gavels, handshakes, targets, sparkles, extra letters, and excessive detail.

Final refinement:

> Keep the document-fold B concept and exact “BidEngine” wordmark, but remove every glow, halo, blur, texture, shadow, bevel, lighting effect, and color-noise artifact. Re-render as flat, razor-sharp vector-like geometry with one uniform warm gold, one off-white wordmark color, and a solid deep navy-black background.

## C — Precision-engine B

Base prompt:

> Create an original precision-engine monogram for BidEngine. Form a capital B from one strong angular spine and two clean calibrated arcs, with a single small score notch suggesting evaluation precision and an engine gauge. Keep it abstract rather than a literal target or speedometer. Use a strong vector-like silhouette, deep navy-black background, warm signal gold, and the exact wordmark “BidEngine”. Keep narrow safe margins and make the mark recognizable at 16px. Avoid checkmarks, document icons, gavels, sparkles, glow, gradients, shadows, and excessive detail.

Final refinement:

> Preserve the forward-leaning angular B and exact “BidEngine” wordmark, but remove every glow, fringe, blur, texture, shadow, bevel, lighting effect, and noisy edge. Re-render as clean flat vector-like geometry with smooth calibrated curves, razor-sharp edges, one subtle measurement notch, uniform gold, off-white wordmark, and a solid deep navy-black background.
